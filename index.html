Synopsis

This course intends to give students fair knowledge of measurement and instrumentation covering measurement standards and units, voltage and current measurement, resistance measurement, DC and AC bridge, transducers and sensors, oscilloscope, and data acquisition (DAQ).

Emphasis is placed on accuracy, precision, resolution, sensitivity, linearity, measurement error, classification of measuring instruments, DC/AC bridge, digital oscilloscope, ADC and DAQ device.
